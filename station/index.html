Hardware — The Station

Every flight starts and ends at the Station.

Where the harness meets the hardware: a standardized stand that tests every drone before it takes off and after it lands — and turns those flights into data.

The idea

The harness gives every drone the same checkout. The Station makes that physical — one stand, every drone, the same tests, every take-off and every landing.

What the Station does

The standardized launch and land point for your fleet.

01

Pre-flight & post-flight tests

The same checkout sequence runs automatically before a drone launches and again when it lands — no clipboards, no skipped steps, no judgment calls on the pad.

02

Performance capture

Every flight is measured against the last one. The Station records how each unit actually performed and feeds it into the harness as characterized data.

03

Provisioning

Power, battery, and fuel are managed at the pad — so the next mission always starts from a known-good state, not a guess.

04

Works across platforms

Built to work with most drone systems being deployed today — including delivery drones. One stand, whatever you fly.

How it connects

The hardware front end of the harness.

The Station doesn't replace the software — it feeds it. Every test it runs and every flight it captures flows straight into the Fleet Performance Harness as standardized, characterized data.

That's what makes a drone trustworthy: not one good flight, but a stand that proves it the same way, every single time.
