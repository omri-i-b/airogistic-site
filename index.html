If you run a drone fleet, you have this problem.

One team builds the drones. Another flies them and trains on them. The hardware has to be hardened and characterized before the AI can do anything with it. Today that handoff is duct tape. We make it standard.

We are picks and shovels, not a mission company. We own everything up to the start of AI training: the checkout, the logs, the data handoff. Not the mission. Not the training. Not where the drones fly.
