What we do

Airogistic is the operations layer for drone fleets — one platform to configure, validate, and monitor every vehicle you fly, so nothing launches without being flight-ready.

Works with the tools you already use

Configure

Push one config to a thousand drones

Define a configuration once and deploy it across your entire fleet in seconds — firmware versions, flight parameters, safety limits, sensor settings.

Every change versioned

Track exactly which vehicle runs which config. Roll back with one click.

Drift detection built in

Know the instant a drone deviates from its golden config — before it causes a failed mission.

Validate

Nothing launches unless it’s flight-ready

Automated pre-flight validation gates every launch. If a vehicle doesn’t meet your safety and regulatory requirements, it stays on the ground.

Immutable audit trail
