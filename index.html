—The Problem

Most swarm program delays aren’t mechanical. They’re procedural.

  • 01

    Configuration drift across the swarm

    Parameters get tweaked on one vehicle, never propagated to the rest. Swarm day comes and the vehicles fly inconsistent configs, or worse, fail their preflight one by one.

  • 02

    Tests that don't repeat

    Manual checklists drift across the fleet. The flight that passed on tail #3 last week fails on tail #7 today and nobody knows what changed.

  • 03

    Field and cloud are split

    Field operators log to spreadsheets. HQ has no swarm-wide view. Nothing lines up when something goes wrong mid-mission.

—The Platform

Swarm, parameters, tests, missions, one system.

01

Swarm registry

Vehicles, ground stations, and types, all tracked. See the whole swarm in one place.

02

Parameter version control

Every flight, every vehicle, pinned to a known-good config. No surprise drift across the swarm.

03

Per-vehicle pre-flight

Block takeoff until every check passes. Sensors, motors, GPS lock, comms, geofence, all scriptable.

04

Swarm-aware in-flight assertions

Telemetry checks that fire mid-mission. Auto-abort or flag anomalies before they propagate.

05

Sim + hardware

Same test definitions run in your simulator and against the live swarm. No translation layer.

06

Swarm mission orchestration

Compose missions as sequences of tests and steps. Coordinate across every vehicle in the swarm.
