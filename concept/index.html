Frequently asked questions

What is the Fleet Performance Harness?

Software that gets every drone flight-ready: standardized pre-flight checkout, a repeatable test runner, AI-debuggable logs, and a clean data handoff from engineering to AI.

Do you make drones?

No. We make the software for drones. We own everything up to takeoff, not the mission.

How is this different from a flight controller or ground station?

A flight controller flies the drone. We make the fleet operable: testing, characterization, logging, and diagnostics across every unit you own.

What platforms does it support?

Built to work with most drone systems being deployed today, including delivery drones. PX4, ArduPilot, and common airframes.

Does our data leave the bench?

Your data stays yours. The harness standardizes and characterizes it so your own engineering and AI teams can actually use it.

How do we get started?

Tell us what you fly and where it hurts. If the harness fits, we get you into an early build.
